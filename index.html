Who We Are

About Our Academy

Knowledge for a Life of Paradise

ABAQER ACADEMY is an Islamic online education academy dedicated to helping students learn and understand Islamic teachings.

Our aim is to provide convenient online learning opportunities for children and adults through structured lessons and dedicated teaching.

We believe that beneficial knowledge helps us grow in faith, character and daily life.

🌙

Our Vision

To nurture a generation illuminated by Islamic knowledge, guided by the Qur'an and Sunnah, and inspired to live a life that seeks Allah's pleasure and the ultimate success of Jannah.

📖

Our Mission

To make authentic Islamic knowledge accessible through quality online education, nurturing strong faith, beautiful character and beneficial knowledge for everyday life.
